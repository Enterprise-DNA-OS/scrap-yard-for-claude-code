# held

Run `npm run scrap -- held`. Fill in the operator's values. Add `--json` when you need every field.

NZ copper is held 14 days from purchase. Victorian goods over 80% copper are held 7 days unless photographed. Nothing on this list is cut, baled or sold before its date. Release with /release once the date passes.

Nothing here sends a message, pays anyone or contacts anyone.
