# Compliance rules this system checks

`/compliance` and the `compliance_findings` view check the records against the rules below. Each rule names its source. Sources were read on 16 November 2026. Laws change: before acting on a finding, check the current wording at the source. Nothing here is legal advice.

| Rule | What the law asks | What a breach looks like in the data | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | No cash for scrap metal. NSW: a dealer must not pay for scrap metal with cash, a cheque payable to cash, or in kind with goods or services. Victoria: a dealer must not pay or receive cash for scrap metal; only a cheque that is not transferable or payable to cash, or an electronic funds transfer. In Victoria, goods with more than 80% copper by weight are second-hand goods, not scrap, and the cash ban does not apply to them. | A paid ticket with `payment_method = cash` at a NSW yard, or at a Victorian yard for any grade at or under 80% copper. `pay` refuses these before they happen. | Scrap Metal Industry Act 2016 (NSW) s 12, [NSW Police scrap metal industry](https://www.police.nsw.gov.au/online_services/scrap_metal_industry); Second-Hand Dealers and Pawnbrokers Act 1989 (Vic), [Consumer Affairs Victoria scrap metal laws](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/second-hand-dealers-and-pawnbrokers/scrap-metal-laws) |
| R02 | Who sold it. NSW transaction records include the seller's identification details and a description and quantity or weight. Victoria: the seller's name, address and identification details, the date and time received. New Zealand: full name, contact address and phone, how identity was verified, date of birth, the seller's signature, a description and quantity, and the date. | A ticket in the last 12 months whose seller has no ID type or number, or no address; at a NZ yard, also no date of birth or no signature on the ticket. `add-ticket` refuses a seller with no ID or address. | Scrap Metal Industry Act 2016 (NSW) s 16; Consumer Affairs Victoria scrap metal laws; Secondhand Dealers and Pawnbrokers Act 2004 (NZ), [NZ Police scrap metal dealers](https://www.police.govt.nz/advice/businesses-and-organisations/secondhand-dealers-and-pawnbrokers/scrap-metal) |
| R03 | A vehicle carries its identifier. NSW records include a vehicle's unique identifiers. Victoria records the vehicle identifier, and a dealer must not buy a vehicle whose identifier has been removed, defaced or altered unless a police officer authorises it in writing. | A line of a vehicle grade with no VIN. `add-ticket` and `add-line` refuse a vehicle with no VIN. | Scrap Metal Industry Act 2016 (NSW) s 16; Consumer Affairs Victoria scrap metal laws |
| R04 | New Zealand copper is an article, not scrap metal. Record the price paid and an item number, label the item until it is sold, and keep it unaltered for 14 days from the transaction where Police can inspect it. | A copper line at a NZ yard released less than 14 days after purchase, or with no item number. `release` refuses inside the 14 days. | NZ Police scrap metal dealers (copper), Secondhand Dealers and Pawnbrokers Act 2004 (NZ) |
| R05 | Victoria: goods over 80% copper are second-hand goods. Keep them 7 days unless a photo is kept. (Scrap metal itself is exempt from the 7-day rule.) | A line over 80% copper at a Victorian yard with no photo, released less than 7 days after purchase. `release` refuses inside the 7 days. | Consumer Affairs Victoria scrap metal laws |
| R06 | Each yard holds a current licence or registration: a secondhand dealer licence in New Zealand, a scrap metal dealer licence in NSW, second-hand dealer registration in Victoria. | A yard with no licence number, no expiry, or an expiry inside 60 days. | Secondhand Dealers and Pawnbrokers Act 2004 (NZ); Scrap Metal Industry Act 2016 (NSW); Second-Hand Dealers and Pawnbrokers Act 1989 (Vic) |

## Not checked yet

- Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania, the ACT and the Northern Territory have their own second-hand dealer and scrap metal rules. Add the ones your yards trade under with `/customise`, with the source, and a test.
- How long records must be kept. Set it for your state or country with `/customise`; this system never deletes a ticket.
- Reporting to police on request. `/draft-register` writes the dealer record for any date range; a person hands it over.

## Changing a rule

Ask in plain words ("our yard in Brisbane needs the Queensland rules") and `/customise` writes the change to the view, this file and the tests together. Do not change a rule without a source.
